How to use this card
01 Lock screen wallpaper: take a screenshot of this card, crop, and set as your phone's lock screen image. Responders see it without unlocking.
02 iOS Medical ID: open Health app → your profile photo → Medical ID → Edit. Mirror blood type, allergies, conditions, and contacts here too.
03 Android Emergency Info: Settings → Safety & Emergency → Emergency Information. Fill in the same critical fields.
04 QR code: any responder can scan the QR with their own phone camera. No app needed — it opens the online health card directly.
